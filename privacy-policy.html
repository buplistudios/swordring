Privacy Policy – Sword Ring

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Sword Ring (“the game”), made by Bupli Studios, handles information.

Information the game stores

Your game progress (swords, Gold, Gems, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interactions. In some regions (such as the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown; you can change this at any time with Settings → Ad privacy options inside the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Play Games (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your game progress is backed up to your Google account using Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can continue on another device. This data is stored by Google under its privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your daily gift is ready). You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The game currently does not sell anything for real money.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete my data / reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game, to delete all game data stored on your device.
